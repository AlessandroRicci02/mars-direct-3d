# Planetary maps

Original, unmodified 2K JPEG maps by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**:

- `2k_earth_clouds.jpg`
- `2k_mars.jpg`
- `2k_sun.jpg`

Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source maps incorporate NASA imagery and elevation data, with artistic color
adjustments. They are visualization maps, not scientific measurement products.
The application changes their rendered appearance through lighting, materials,
and color management; the downloaded files are unchanged.

## Earth surface

`earth_atmos_2048.jpg` is the unmodified Earth map distributed with the Three.js examples:
https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg
